FEB 2024 – PRESENT

Lead UX/UI Designer · Cyberstar Software

Sole designer for compliance software used by Department of Defense agencies to manage cybersecurity workforce qualification under the DoD Cyber Workforce Framework (DCWF 8140/8570).

  • Leading the redesign of the Management Dashboard, the main tool compliance leaders use to see who isn't qualified and report readiness to agency and CIO leadership.
  • Runs research with agency compliance leaders through interviews, contextual inquiry, support tickets and usage data. It showed that a simple compliant/non-compliant split didn't match how managers triage, which led to a rebuild around a multi-tier qualification taxonomy.
  • Facilitates cross-functional design studios and uses assumption mapping and an evidence-vs-importance matrix to cut scope down to what six engineers can build.
  • Tests rough concepts with customers before any high-fidelity work, and ties each design decision to a specific research finding.
  • Set the design principles and built a tokenized design system and component library so every screen in the product shares one look.
  • Designs to Section 508, showing status with labels and counts as well as color so it works for colorblind and screen reader users.
  • Builds clickable prototypes in Figma and Claude Design that stakeholders and engineers can inspect directly, so less gets lost at handoff.
  • Audited the whole application and turned the findings into a prioritized UX backlog for engineering. Also helps marketing with conferences, outreach and sales demos.
